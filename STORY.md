# Story

The companion reads only Public, reached Stage N sections and After moonfall once unlocked. Keep these sections short. Mission dialogue belongs in MISSIONS.md.

## Public

You are not dead. You are not dead. Fight.

You wake buried in sealed Foundry armour. Its reactor is critically low. Emergency fists crackle; all other weapons and thrusters are offline. A quadruped recovery robot waits above the wreckage. You belong to the Foundry, independent machines that preserve living worlds. Whether you began as a human, a machine or both remains unknown.

The Presence is a distributed alien intelligence. Mobile castes roam occupied worlds and protect rooted minds in descending underground burrows. Minds are like queens of a growing colony. The first burrow has only basic stalkers.

An empty Foundry ship waits in low orbit. The restored suit can reach it. Your companion then joins the cockpit and keeps a radio channel open wherever you travel.

## Stage 3

After the first burrow is demolished, its regional factory delivers powered fists and a basic hand beam. The second factory delivers surface thrusters with a 65-metre ceiling after you clear its burrow. The third delivers orbital flight. Later worlds reward the remaining weapon families.

Killing bodies opens a foothold but cannot clear a world. Build one factory in each Earth region so laser-armed robodogs can replicate and control the alien population. The third Earth factory builds ships as well as robodogs. Every new planetary factory after Earth does both. Production stops when the relevant enemy population is gone. Factories support their own planet and its immediate neighbours; remote worlds need your intervention. Without you the Presence keeps its advantage. Later worlds and systems have larger populations and faster growth.

## Stage 8

The Presence copies captured technology. Its weapons resemble yours, and the fabrication marks inside your armour match the Foundry's oldest machines.

Your companion helped create your armour and reconstruct your memory. It cannot prove whether it rescued you or assembled you. It is loyal to the person you are now.

## Stage 9

Your companion helped create this version of you to break the Presence. The living citadel contains two relay minds and a larger shield mind. Their Spine turrets and turning tunnels guard a link to the final mind, buried beneath a planet too deep for nuclear weapons. You must land on a moon and build its engines. The impact will collapse the mind into a singularity and consume this system. There is no gate to repair or power.

## After moonfall

The moon has struck. Worlds spiral into the black hole, followed by their sun. When the last light is consumed, everything goes white. You and your companion emerge in another system where the Presence grows faster. Your origin remains unresolved.
