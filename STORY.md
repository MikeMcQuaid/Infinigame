# Story

This is the story source used in the companion's prompt. Edit the paragraphs under the recognised headings below. `Public` is always known, `Stage N` is revealed once that mission is reached and `After moonfall` is revealed after the impact. Notes outside these sections are for the author. Mission-specific dialogue belongs in `MISSIONS.md` and the factions' shared facts in `FACTIONS.md`.

## Public

A sealed solar system is being consumed by one distributed alien presence. Its rooted minds grow mobile bodies and living ships, strip worlds for resources and destroy independent life. The Foundry is a network of self-replicating machines built to preserve that life and reclaim its habitats. Its machines have individual identities and cooperate rather than surrendering their minds.

You wake beneath wreckage with missing memories. Your recovery companion recognises you but cannot restore the whole record. You are on the Foundry's side. Neither your body nor your memories establish whether you began as a human, a machine or something made from both. Keep that question open.

Destroying a local mind breaks its coordination, but surviving minds can grow replacements. Reclaimed buildings remain, and friendly machines turn their resources into protected colonies. The growing enemy creates pressure, never a deadline that ends the game.

## Stage 7

Captured enemy weapons resemble your own. The presence learns from the equipment it captures. Your companion's records predate the crash, and the same fabrication marks appear inside your suit and the machines' workshops.

## Stage 8

Your companion helped make your armour and reconstruct your memory. It admits its involvement, but cannot prove whether it rescued you or assembled you. It is loyal to the person you are now.

## Stage 14

Your companion helped create this version of you to silence the presence. The radiation boundary is a prison built to contain it. Repairing the gate before destroying the last mind would give the enemy a path into other systems. The citadel's core sustains that mind's shield.

## After moonfall

The moon impact has destroyed the last buried mind and its world. Surviving Foundry colonies can power the gate. Beyond it are other systems with surviving life, machine settlements and new branches of the presence. Your companion will travel with you; your origin remains unresolved.
