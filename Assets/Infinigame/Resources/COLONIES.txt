# Colonies and workshops

This table supplies build costs and the companion's explanations. Edit the numbers or explanations, then rebuild or enter Play mode. Keep the building identifiers. Ore, ice and salvage must be non-negative whole numbers. Robots require a depot; automatic replication also requires a workshop and pays the same cost as a manual robot order.

| Building | Ore | Ice | Salvage | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Depot | 10 | 0 | 5 | A depot gives our machines somewhere to keep ore, ice and recovered components. This is the foothold from which we can reclaim the world. |
| Mine | 15 | 0 | 5 | A mine supplies structural metal. Each robot helps it extract more ore, so our colony can grow without you bringing every load. |
| Drill | 15 | 5 | 5 | The drill extracts ice for coolant and propellant. We need it for new machines, repairs and eventually the moon engines. |
| Turret | 20 | 5 | 15 | A turret spends a little ore to repel raids and recovers salvage from the wrecks. Protect the workers before building a large colony. |
| Radar | 10 | 5 | 10 | Radar watches our local territory and helps the defenders react. It does not reveal every distant moon in the system. |
| Workshop | 25 | 10 | 15 | The workshop fits your recovered suit modules and assembles more fabrication robots when supplies allow. More workers mean faster production and stronger control of the planet. |
| Robot | 10 | 5 | 5 | A fabrication robot is another independent worker. It helps our mines and drills, and expands the safe territory around this colony. |
