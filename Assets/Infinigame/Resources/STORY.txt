# Story

The companion reads only Public, reached Stage N sections and After moonfall once unlocked. Keep these sections short. Mission dialogue belongs in MISSIONS.md.

## Public

You are not dead. You are not dead. Fight.

You wake buried in sealed Foundry armour. Its reactor is critically low. Emergency fists crackle; all other weapons and thrusters are offline. A quadruped recovery robot waits above the wreckage. You belong to the Foundry, independent machines that preserve living worlds. Whether you began as a human, a machine or both remains unknown.

The Presence is a distributed alien intelligence. Mobile castes roam occupied worlds and protect rooted minds in descending underground burrows. Minds are like queens of a growing colony. The first burrow has only basic stalkers. Its power core restores the suit's thrusters, impact drive, hand beams, shoulder missiles, rail gauntlet and hemisphere lasers.

An empty Foundry ship waits in low orbit. The restored suit can reach it. Your companion then joins the cockpit and keeps a radio channel open wherever you travel.

Killing bodies opens a foothold but cannot clear a world. Seed a factory so laser-armed robodogs can replicate and hunt. Once a factory conquers its world, upgrade it to a shipyard to contest the living fleet. Production stops when the relevant enemy population is gone. Factories support their own planet and its immediate neighbours; remote worlds need your intervention. Without you the Presence keeps its advantage. Later worlds and systems have larger populations and faster growth.

## Stage 7

The Presence copies captured technology. Its weapons resemble yours, and the fabrication marks inside your armour match the Foundry's oldest machines.

## Stage 8

Your companion helped create your armour and reconstruct your memory. It cannot prove whether it rescued you or assembled you. It is loyal to the person you are now.

## Stage 11

Your companion helped create this version of you to break the Presence. The citadel's shield core protects a final mind buried too deep for nuclear weapons. Your factories must build moon engines. The impact will collapse the mind into a singularity and consume this system. There is no gate to repair or power.

## After moonfall

The moon has struck. Worlds spiral into the black hole, followed by their sun. When the last light is consumed, everything goes white. You and your companion emerge in another system where the Presence grows faster. Your origin remains unresolved.
