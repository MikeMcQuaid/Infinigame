# Missions

This table is the campaign source. Edit names, objectives, briefings, guard counts and rewards here, then run `script/play` or enter Play mode in Unity. The editor packages it into the game. Tests read this same file.

Keep the stage numbers 0–11 and their order. Save version 2 migrates the earlier 15-mission campaign. `World` uses a first-system world name; generated systems map the later missions onto their own worlds. `Guard` uses `FoeKind` names and `Rewards` uses `Gear` names. Stages 0–4 stay on the home world. Minds live in descending burrows with ground guards. Stage 3 requires a robodog factory; stage 4 requires boarding the unmanned ship in orbit. Rewards are comma-separated. Do not put a vertical bar inside a cell.

The recovery tutorial unlocks punching, looking and walking in that order, then spawns one ground fighter. Equipment pickups arm a 30-second nuclear demolition; escape at least 65 m from the mind. Two guards stay by every mind. From Luna onwards, destroy the outer turret and flying ship before attacking the mind; later bases add more of both. The first journeys stay within 300 m. The station has six corners and a 25-second escape alarm. Shipyards require ground victory; moon engines require local fleet victory.
