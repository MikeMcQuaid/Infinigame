# Missions

The first three burrows divide Earth into regions, with one, two and three linked minds respectively. Every mind in a region must fall before its robots reach full strength. Each recovered core starts a twenty-second escape. When its burrow explodes, the core brings its equipment online. The first explosion also releases one suit level. Choose an improvement, then build a robodog factory in the cleared region to advance. Later surface missions accept an existing regional factory. Death before demolition restarts the current burrow. The citadel gives twenty-five seconds to escape.
