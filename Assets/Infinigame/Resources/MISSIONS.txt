# Missions

The first three burrows divide Earth into regions, with one, two and three linked minds respectively. Every mind in a region must fall before its robots reach full strength. Arming a burrow’s demolition charge starts a twenty-second escape. After the explosion, build a robodog factory in the cleared region and collect the recovered equipment beside it. The first collection also releases one suit level. At the last burrow on each world the local factory must become a shipyard before it releases the equipment. Later surface missions accept an existing regional factory. Death before demolition restarts the current burrow. The citadel gives twenty-five seconds to escape.
