# Missions

The first three burrows divide Earth into regions, with one, two and three linked minds respectively. Every mind in a region must fall before its robots reach full strength. Each power core unlocks one step of suit recovery: powered fists and beam, surface flight limited to 65 metres, then orbital flight. Every region needs its own factory before Earth is controlled. Death restarts the current burrow. Burrow demolition gives twenty seconds to escape; the citadel retains its twenty-five-second escape.
