# Replication factories

After recovering the first burrow’s power core, use the factory uplink while on the surface. Earth has three regions, with one, two and three linked minds, separate alien populations and a factory each. All minds in a region must fall to release the robodogs’ full strength. A robodog factory requires 4 local alien kills, plus one per world difficulty rank and two per additional system depth. Killing the local mind also counts. Tab shows kills required, remaining aliens, robodogs and both fleets.

A factory begins with eight robodogs, plus four per world difficulty rank. One machine emerges immediately from the factory’s assembly lift, then another every second and a half, up to seven nearby robodogs. Their patrol radius widens with the regional population. The factory replaces losses and builds towards a standing pack. Near the player, up to five roaming aliens can skirmish around the factory alongside the existing mission defenders. Their dorsal lasers and the factory's rotating multiweapon mount protect the foothold. The mount cycles an impact pulse, beam, guided missiles, rail shot and hemisphere sweep. There are no separate turrets, radar towers, mines or workshops to build.

An active mind suppresses robot strength and replication: early patrols can be wiped out. After you kill the mind, an intact factory always rebuilds from zero and eventually wins the ground war, at every difficulty. The brood keeps hatching underground. Its surviving clutches supply the patrols with new opponents even after the pack controls the region.

A shipyard is an upgrade to that same factory, available only after its robots conquer the whole world. The shipyard is roughly twice the factory’s footprint. Upgrading immediately puts one actual robot-piloted ship on the assembly lift. It rises, powers up and follows you by default, including during autopilot. Fleet orders starts at one escort; request more as the fleet grows. They replicate while hostile ships remain. Later fronts can overwhelm a young factory without the player's help.

Use **Fleet orders** in the pause menu to choose one to three escorts. **Follow** holds formation and defends against nearby ships, **Attack** engages hostile ships within 2.5 km and **Patrol** returns them to their shipyard. Escorts keep up with autopilot and survive travel between worlds. This small personal wing can travel with you; autonomous population expansion remains limited to neighbouring planets.

Nearby surface patrols gather around your factories while aliens remain, and hostile ships contest the deployed robot fleet. These are actual combatants using the shared damage and shield rules. Once a front is conquered, its population stops spawning enemies.

Only player-established shipyards send neighbouring support. They cannot relay it further. A distant planet requires another foothold. Populations are saved and advance during active play, not while paused or while the game is closed.

After all three citadel minds fall, a shipyard that has also cleared its local hostile fleet can build moon engines. The engines assemble, ignite and send the moon towards the buried mind while your ship follows at a safe distance. Impact starts the singularity ending automatically. Choose **Moon engines built · replay finale** from the cheats mission picker to supply the prerequisites and immediately start the whole sequence. Choose it again to restart the animation.
