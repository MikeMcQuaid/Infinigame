# Replication factories

Only two facilities can be built. Press B while on foot with suit power restored. A dog factory requires 4 local alien kills, plus one per world difficulty rank and two per additional system depth. Killing the local mind also counts. There are no ore, ice or salvage building costs, turrets, radar, mines or separate workshops.

A dog factory begins with eight laser dogs, plus four per world difficulty rank. Every dog helps build more dogs while aliens remain. Production stops once that world's alien population reaches zero. A ship factory requires a dog factory on a moon. It begins with two autonomous ships and follows the same rule for the enemy fleet. Later fronts may overwhelm a new factory without the player's help.

Only player-established ship factories send neighbouring support. They cannot relay that support further. A remote planet requires another foothold. Factory populations are saved, and simulation advances during active play rather than while the game is closed.

The identifiers below are used by the content importer. Costs are zero because access is earned in combat. Explanations are available to the companion.

| Building | Ore | Ice | Salvage | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Factory | 0 | 0 | 0 | Clear a foothold, then seed a factory. Its laser dogs replicate and hunt until this world is safe. |
| ShipFactory | 0 | 0 | 0 | On a moon, our dogs can assemble a ship factory. Its ships contest the living fleet and support one neighbouring planet. |

After the citadel shield core falls, a factory with 16 dogs can build moon engines. The resulting impact starts the singularity ending automatically. There is no gate-building step.
