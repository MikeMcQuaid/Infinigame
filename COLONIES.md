# Replication factories

After recovering the second burrow’s surface thrusters, use the factory uplink while on the surface. Earth has three regions, with one, two and three linked minds, separate alien populations and a factory each. All minds in a region must fall to release the robodogs’ full strength. A robodog factory requires 4 local alien kills, plus one per world difficulty rank and two per additional system depth. Killing the local mind also counts. Tab shows kills required, remaining aliens, robodogs and both fleets.

A factory begins with eight robodogs, plus four per world difficulty rank. One machine emerges immediately from the factory’s assembly lift, then another every three seconds, up to seven nearby robodogs. Their patrol radius widens with the regional population. Each contributes to exponential replication while aliens remain. Their dorsal lasers and the factory's rotating multiweapon mount protect the foothold. The mount cycles an impact pulse, beam, guided missiles, rail shot and hemisphere sweep. Production stops once the region’s alien population reaches zero. There are no separate turrets, radar towers, mines or workshops to build.

An active mind suppresses robot strength and replication: early patrols can be wiped out. After you kill the mind, an intact factory always rebuilds from zero and eventually wins the ground war, at every difficulty. Remaining aliens no longer replicate. You do not need to keep rebuilding the factory.

A shipyard is an upgrade to that same factory, available only after its robots conquer the whole world. The shipyard is roughly twice the factory’s footprint. Its assembly lift raises an actual robot-piloted ship, which powers up and joins the fleet, starting with two ships. They replicate while hostile ships remain. Later fronts can overwhelm a young factory without the player's help.

Only player-established shipyards send neighbouring support. They cannot relay it further. A distant planet requires another foothold. Populations are saved and advance during active play, not while paused or while the game is closed.

After all three citadel minds fall, a shipyard that has also cleared its local hostile fleet can build moon engines. The engines assemble, ignite and send the moon towards the buried mind while your ship follows at a safe distance. Impact starts the singularity ending automatically. Choose **Moon engines built · replay finale** from the cheats mission picker to supply the prerequisites and immediately start the whole sequence. Choose it again to restart the animation.
